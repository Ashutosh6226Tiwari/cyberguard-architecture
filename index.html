CyberGuard Architecture

CyberGuard is a native Android link-safety firewall that inspects external URLs before they ever open in your browser. By intercepting ACTION_VIEW and ACTION_SEND intents, CyberGuard evaluates links across 15 on-device lexical heuristics, Google DNS-over-HTTPS, and ICANN RDAP domain registration age without ever loading the target page—delivering zero-fetch protection against mobile phishing.
